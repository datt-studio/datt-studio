Four recognitions from one submission to CSS Design Awards. One came from their crew and judges. Three came from the public.

## Special Kudos

Special Kudos is described by CSS Design Awards as a professional mark of recognition for exemplary work, granted irrespective of winning Website of the Day. It is not a category and it is not a placing. It is a decision to name the work, made by the people who run the awards.

We are proud of it. We built this studio on the belief that purposeful design needs rigorous strategy and absolute restraint, and a mark of recognition for exactly that is the one we wanted.

## Best UI Design, Best UX Design and Best Innovation

The same submission was then voted by the public into three categories. UI Design, UX Design and Innovation, each won on votes cast by the people who came to the site and looked at it.

Four recognitions, two entirely different processes. An international panel and a crew made a judgement about the work. A readership made three. Being recognised across those disciplines by both is a milestone we do not take lightly.

## What was submitted

The site. Its description, its own words, and nothing else. No case study, no client list, no process notes, no metrics. The Agency category entry pointed at a live URL and let the work carry the claim on its own.

Everything the judges and the public responded to was the thing itself.

## Reaching the mark

In Vietnamese, Đạt translates to the act of reaching, attaining and fulfilling a standard. We did not set out to win an award. We set a standard at the start of the project and held to it, and the recognition came from people who had no reason to give it.

The bar is set. We are moving forward, focusing on what comes next.

## Thank you

To the judges for the rigorous evaluation, and to everyone who voted for us.

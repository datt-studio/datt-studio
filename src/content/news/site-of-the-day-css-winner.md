Site of the Day at CSS Winner, 1 October 2026. Selected by their jury.

## The score

The jury scored the submission on four criteria, and chose it from the websites in the final round against the same four.

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---|
| Design | 7.6 |
| Functionality | 7.6 |
| Usability | 7.7 |
| Content | 7.7 |
| **Overall** | **7.7** |

A site that leads on video, typography and restraint, scored highest in usability and content. That is the part we will hold on to. The two marks below it are on design and functionality, and they are the two we have the most to keep improving.

## What was submitted

DATT STUDIO LTD, United Kingdom. Category: DesignAgency. Tags: video, typography, minimalist, full screen, animated.

The submission carried 66 likes from the community that week.

Nothing was submitted to earn it. No case study, no metrics, no process notes. The entry pointed at a live URL and the site did the rest.

## Site of the Month

Site of the Day qualifies the site for the Site of the Month contest, which runs on the same four criteria and closes at the end of the month. It is still open. We will say so here if it goes further.

## Thank you

To the panel for the recognition, and to everyone who came to the site and voted for us.

The bar is set. We are moving forward, keeping our focus exactly where it belongs.
